# Discovery meeting questions

Use these questions to find the owner's biggest bottleneck and to fill in `EXP-001` in [`experiment-ledger.csv`](experiment-ledger.csv). Ask about what happened recently, not about what he would do with a tool that does not exist yet. Do not demo the starter app until the last section. A demo before then steers him toward service request intake.

## His week

1. Walk me through last week. Where did your time go?
2. Which task did you or your office repeat the most?
3. What fell behind or slipped last month? What did it cost you?
4. If you had 5 more hours next week, what would you spend them on?

## His current system

5. Why did you reject Centerpoint Connect? What did it get wrong?
6. What lives in SharePoint today: lists, files, or both? Who updates them?
7. Which parts of the work happen outside SharePoint, in email, spreadsheets, phone calls, or paper?

## The workflow he ranks first

8. Show me the last time you did it, from the first email or call to the finished result.
9. How many times a week does it happen? How long does each one take, and who does it?
10. Where does it get stuck or need rework?
11. Which steps need your judgment, and which steps are copying information from one place to another?
12. Who must approve the result before it goes to a customer or a crew?

## Data and permissions

13. Can you share 10 recent real examples, with customer details removed, within 2 weeks?
14. Who in your company would need to agree before a tool reads your SharePoint or mailbox?
15. Is there any information we must never see or store?

## Close

16. If we take one recent example and do it by hand with an AI draft, will you review the draft and tell us how close it is?
17. What would make you stop using a tool like this after a week?

## After the meeting

Fill in the `baseline` and `result` columns for `EXP-001` the same day. Write the `decision` column as persevere or pivot, with the reason in one sentence.
